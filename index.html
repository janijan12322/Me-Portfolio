About me

Hi, I’m Kashif,
a developer who loves
clean, fast stores.

I’m a Shopify developer based in Islamabad, Pakistan, with 1.5+ years of experience across Shopify and WooCommerce. I build custom Liquid themes with schema-driven sections, dynamic templates and metafields, so merchants can manage their own content without waiting on a developer.

Before Shopify I worked as a WordPress developer and as a UI/UX designer and frontend developer. That mix means I care about how a page looks, how it feels on a phone and how quickly it loads. I studied Computer Science at GC University Faisalabad.
